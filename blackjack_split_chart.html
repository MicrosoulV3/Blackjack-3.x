Blackjack Split Chart

Quick reference for deciding when to split a pair.

Important: The game only offers Split when the two dealt cards are the same rank, such as 8 + 8, K + K, or Q + Q. Cards that merely share the same point value, such as Q + 10, are not treated as a pair.
